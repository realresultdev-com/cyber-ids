🚀 3. How to Run the Project (One-Click)
1. Extract the ZIP:
 Right-click Network_Intrusion_Detection_Project.zip → select "Extract All...".
2. Launch:
 Open the extracted folder and double-click start.bat.
3. Automatic Setup:
 The script handles everything automatically:
   * Configures the Python virtual environment.
   * Installs PyTorch, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, and FastAPI.
   * Installs frontend packages (npm install).
   * Seeds the database and pre-trained models.
   * Starts both servers and launches your browser at http://localhost:5173.
(To stop the application at any time, simply double-click stop.bat).

6. Quick Viva Q&A
      * Q: Why use the CICIDS2017 dataset?
 A: It models modern realistic network traffic with natural class imbalance (85%+ Benign vs rare attacks) across 78 extracted flow features.
      * Q: Why is Accuracy misleading for intrusion detection?
 A: A model predicting 100% Benign will score 85%+ accuracy but miss every single attack. Macro-F1 and Minority Recall are the essential metrics.
      * Q: How does packet-to-flow aggregation work?
 A: Packets are aggregated by their 5-tuple: (Source IP, Destination IP, Source Port, Destination Port, Protocol). When completed, statistical vectors are evaluated by the ML/DL pipeline.
